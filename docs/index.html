Confidently wrong about the long tail

Four large language models answered 220 questions about 110 ordinary museum objects, in English, German, Spanish and Greek. Without the museum's record, one answer in three stated something false or undocumented. With the record, most errors disappeared, except where the record was silent.

Finding 1 · grounding

The record removes the errors it can answer

Closed-book, the models differed sharply. With the record in their context, hallucination on answerable questions fell below 3% for every model. On questions the record cannot answer, the improvement was much smaller.

Finding 2 · popularity

Fame matters more than language

On average the visitor's language made no difference (odds ratio for Greek vs English 1.07). Fame did. In the National Gallery in Athens, with the same thin metadata, closed-book answers about famous paintings were correct 48% of the time and about unknown ones 17%.

Finding 3 · anatomy

The record often supplies the error

Most hallucinations given with the record were not inventions. A name in the creator field became the maker; a period label became a date. One model reports the field with its role, another turns it into the answer.

Finding 4 · exploratory follow-up

Say what is not known, and the models say it too

We added one line to each record ("Maker: not recorded in this record.") and labelled fields for what they are. On the same 57 unanswerable questions, hallucination fell from 18.4% to 2.5%, for every model.

Tool · explicit-record

Make absence explicit in your own records

Paste a record with one Label: value per line. The tool adds an absence line for every empty core field and, if you choose, relabels fields whose content is not the maker or a date of making. It runs in your browser and calls no model; it is the same rule set as the Python tool released with the article.

Amended record

Tested in the study only for the questions asked and only on unanswerable questions. Test on your own collection, including questions the record can answer, before deployment.
